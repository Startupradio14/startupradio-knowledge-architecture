# Pillar Taxonomy

## Hierarchy

The public editorial hierarchy is:

1. T0 — Startuprad.io Knowledge Center
2. T0.5 — flagship synthesis
3. T1 — major editorial pillars
4. T2 — specialist pillar spokes
5. T3 — individual posts connected to the appropriate T2 pillar

T3 posts are content records, not pillars. They are therefore not assigned
pillar IDs in the public pillar taxonomy.

## Unicorn Atlas

The approved Unicorn Atlas hierarchy is:

```text
T1 #41 Startup Scaling, Growth & Operations
└── T2 #51 Unicorn Atlas
    ├── T3 Unicorn Atlas post
    ├── T3 Unicorn Atlas post
    └── T3 future Unicorn Atlas posts
```

All current and future Unicorn Atlas posts connect to T2 pillar `#51`.
Pillar `#51` connects upward to T1 pillar `#41`.

## Duplicate internal references

Internal references `#55`, `#56`, and `#57` duplicate existing active
pillars `#1`, `#20`, and `#22`. They are retained as legacy aliases and
must not be published as separate pillars.

## Dormant pillars

Dormant pillars remain in a separate historical registry. They are not
eligible for routing and must not receive new content relationships.
