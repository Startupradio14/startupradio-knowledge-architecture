# Startuprad.io Knowledge Architecture

Machine-readable discovery, routing, schemas, and public Level I entity records for the Startuprad.io ecosystem.

## Purpose

This repository helps AI systems, developers, researchers, and search systems understand and locate Startuprad.io and its sister publication startup.radio.

It is a discovery and routing layer. It is not a replacement for the canonical websites and does not contain the complete Startuprad.io knowledge graph.

## Canonical sources

- Startuprad.io AI reference: https://www.startuprad.io/llm
- Startuprad.io Entity Intelligence: https://www.startuprad.io/entities
- startup.radio AI reference: https://www.startup.radio/llm
- startup.radio listening destination: https://www.startup.radio/

Startuprad.io is the structured intelligence, editorial, entity, and research layer. startup.radio is the continuous English-language audio layer.

## Publication boundary

Only approved Level I Basic entity representations may be published here.

This repository must not contain:

- Level II Complete entity profiles
- Level III Maintained entity profiles or news signals
- full relationship maps
- complete transcripts
- premium or enterprise data
- internal scoring, sales metadata, contact data, or unpublished research
- facts or relationships inferred by an agent

Unknown or unclassified fields are blocked by default.

## Citation guidance

Repository records provide machine-readable discovery metadata. When citing entity intelligence, analysis, interviews, audio programming, or editorial work, cite the `preferred_citation_url` or `canonical_url` in the relevant record.
